# Special prize scout — GEMS-Taiwan 2026

Where GEMS-Taiwan stands in the iGEM 2026 high-school section: the **finalist**
race (2 slots among 147 teams) and its three special prize nominations,
**Measurement**, **Hardware** and **Integrated Human Practices**, after the wiki freeze.

## Finalist read (23 October)

| Rank | Team | Panel score (0.75 Project + 0.25 Wiki) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HongKong-JSS | 4.24 |
| 2 = | **GEMS-Taiwan** | 4.17 |
| 2 = | Korea-HS | 4.17 |
| 4 | Labi-Taiwan | 4.14 |
| 5 | SMS-Shenzhen | 4.12 |

Three independent judges, each trained on the 2026 Judge Handbook, read the full
wikis of the 24 teams that passed two gates. GEMS-Taiwan ties Korea-HS for the second
slot. An adversarial reviewer checked the decisive claims against the wikis. With its
corrections applied, Korea-HS and SMS-Shenzhen both move ahead of us and we drop to fourth,
mainly because our Results page says green light switches ACCD on and our own
figures do not show that. A finalist place is possible, not the likely outcome.
Presentation videos and the Q&A were not assessed. Full method, per-judge scores and
the whole field are on the Finalist tab; the reusable dataset is in [`field/`](field/).

## Special prizes (22 October)

Every high-school team with real content on `/measurement`, `/hardware` or
`/human-practices` (and their sub-pages) was scored against the 2026 Judging
Handbook, 0–5 per aspect. The leading Hardware and IHP entries were then
re-judged side by side in a finals pass.

| Prize | Our rank | Score | Leader |
|---|---|---|---|
| Measurement | **1st of 38** | 18.5 / 20 | us; Korea-HS 17.0 |
| Hardware | **2nd of 44** (tied on points) | 17.5 / 20 | Lambert-GA 17.5 |
| Integrated HP | **9th of 104** | 26.5 / 30 | MammothEdu-United 28.5 |

- **Measurement** is the strongest card: 1.5 points clear. The half point lost
  since 21 October (19.0 → 18.5) is the single-well biology, not the photometer.
- **Hardware** is a coin flip. The tie-break goes to Lambert-GA on user testing:
  two outside labs ran its device; ours was rebuilt by classmates.
- **IHP** is a top-ten finish in a field where ten teams sit within two points.
  The page promises three downloadable resource packages and links none.

14 teams with Human Practices pages were not scored because one judge pass ended
early; they are listed on the page. Part Collection was ruled out on 21 October
(22nd of 36) and lives in this repository's history.

## Pages

Six pages share one tab bar, in this order:

| Tab | File | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| GEMS Taiwan overview | `index.html` | Finalist outlook card, the recommendation, the three prize summaries (each links to its tab), the conclusion, the Before Paris list and the scoring method |
| Hardware | `hardware.html` | Hardware board: top 10 with per-criterion notes, top-15 chart, criteria |
| Measurement | `measurement.html` | Measurement board, same layout |
| Integrated Human Practices | `ihp.html` | IHP board, same layout |
| Traditional powers | `traditional.html` | Why GreatBay-SCIE (12th) and Lambert-GA (17th) finished outside the top ten: aspect-by-aspect gaps against the three teams above them, what would move each one, and how each judge summed them up |
| Finalist | `finalist.html` | Finalist read: verdict, our position, the deep-judged ranking with per-judge scores and aspect bars, sensitivity, method, gates, judges, and the whole field with search and gate filter |

Every page is bilingual (English / 繁體中文). The language choice carries across tabs
(stored in `localStorage`; `?lang=zh` also works). Old single-page links such as
`index.html#hardware` forward to the matching tab, and a board row can be opened
directly with `#<prize>-<slug>`, for example `measurement.html#measurement-gems-taiwan`
or `finalist.html#rank-gems-taiwan`.

## Layout

```
assets/site.css     shared styles (design, tab bar, finalist page)
assets/site.js      shared helpers, language toggle, prize boards, overview
assets/finalist.js  finalist page renderer
data/measurement.js window.SCOUT_MEASUREMENT = [...]  every scored team, ranked
data/hardware.js    window.SCOUT_HARDWARE    = [...]
data/ihp.js         window.SCOUT_IHP         = [...]
data/concl.js       window.SCOUT_CONCL       = {...}  overview and conclusion text
data/finalist.js    window.SCOUT_FINALIST    = {...}  finalist read, generated by field/scripts/build_finalist.py
data/traditional.js window.SCOUT_TRADITIONAL = {...}  GreatBay-SCIE and Lambert-GA, generated by field/scripts/build_traditional.py
field/              reusable field dataset for all 147 high-school teams (see field/README.md)
```

The data files are plain `<script src>` files, not JSON fetched at run time, so the
pages work from `file://` as well as on GitHub Pages. To update a board, replace the
array in its data file; the pages need no change. `data/finalist.js` and `data/traditional.js` are generated; do not hand-edit them. The
traditional-powers prose lives in `field/traditional_prose.json`; edit that and re-run
`python3 field/scripts/build_traditional.py` from the repo root to recompute every score,
gap and ranking in the page (see field/README.md).

Scores are our own reading of public pages against the handbook, not iGEM's: special prizes taken 22 October 2026, finalist read 23 October 2026.
