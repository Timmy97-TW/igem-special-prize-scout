Recommendation建議

The judging criteria評審標準

Each award is scored on the aspects the 2026 Judging Handbook lists for it, 0–5 each. Measurement and Hardware have four aspects (out of 20); Integrated Human Practices has six (out of 30).每個獎項依 2026 評審手冊列出的面向評分，每個面向 0–5 分。量測與硬體各四個面向（滿分 20）；整合人文考量有六個面向（滿分 30）。

Measurement量測獎 Measurement

  1. Could the measurement be repeated by other iGEM teams?其他 iGEM 隊伍能否重複這項量測？
  2. Is the protocol well described?實驗流程是否描述完整？
  3. Is it useful to other projects?對其他專題是否有用？
  4. Did the team use controls to validate the process and calibrate units?是否以對照組驗證量測流程並校正單位？

Evidence must be on /measurement. Model entry: Austin-UTexas 2024.證據必須放在 /measurement。範例：Austin-UTexas 2024。

Hardware硬體獎 Hardware

  1. Does the hardware address a need or problem in synthetic biology?硬體是否解決合成生物學中的需求或問題？
  2. Did the team conduct user testing and learn from user feedback?是否進行使用者測試並從回饋中學習？
  3. Did the team demonstrate utility and functionality in a proof of concept?是否以概念驗證展示硬體的實用性與功能？
  4. Is the documentation enough for other teams to reproduce it?文件是否足以讓其他隊伍重製？

Standard page /hardware, sub-pages count. Reference winners: TUDarmstadt 2025, Aachen 2024.標準頁面 /hardware，子頁面也算。參考得獎隊伍：TUDarmstadt 2025、Aachen 2024。

Integrated Human Practices整合人文考量 IHP

  1. Was HP integrated throughout the project?人文考量是否貫穿整個專案？
  2. How thoughtfully was it implemented: context, rationale, prior work?執行是否用心：脈絡、理由與先前工作？
  3. Did it make the project responsible and good for the world?是否讓專案更負責任、對世界有益？
  4. Did it incorporate different stakeholder views?是否納入不同利害關係人的觀點？
  5. Is it documented so others can build on it?文件是否足以讓別人在其上發展？
  6. How inspiring an example is it to others?對其他人是否是有啟發性的範例？

The test across all six: what a stakeholder said, what the team changed, and what they checked afterwards.六個面向共同的檢驗：利害關係人說了什麼、隊伍改了什麼、之後又驗證了什麼。
