Integrated Human Practices整合人文考量 IHP

  1. Was HP integrated throughout the project?人文考量是否貫穿整個專案？
  2. How thoughtfully was it implemented: context, rationale, prior work?執行是否用心：脈絡、理由與先前工作？
  3. Did it make the project responsible and good for the world?是否讓專案更負責任、對世界有益？
  4. Did it incorporate different stakeholder views?是否納入不同利害關係人的觀點？
  5. Is it documented so others can build on it?文件是否足以讓別人在其上發展？
  6. How inspiring an example is it to others?對其他人是否是有啟發性的範例？

The test across all six: what a stakeholder said, what the team changed, and what they checked afterwards.六個面向共同的檢驗：利害關係人說了什麼、隊伍改了什麼、之後又驗證了什麼。

Prepared for GEMS-Taiwan's internal nomination decision. Scores are our own reading of public pages against the handbook, not iGEM's. 供 GEMS-Taiwan 內部決定提名使用。分數是我們依評審手冊閱讀公開頁面後的判斷，並非 iGEM 官方評分。
